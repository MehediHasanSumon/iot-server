These URLs can be called directly by your IoT microcontroller or curl for quick testing:

GET /api/devices (All status)
GET /api/devices/1?format=plain (Raw "on" / "off")
GET /api/devices/1/set/on (Direct Turn ON)
GET /api/devices/1/set/off (Direct Turn OFF)
POST /api/devices/1/toggle (Toggle state)
